Credit decisioning

Approve more of the right borrowers, and explain every decline.

Gradient-boosted scorecards trained on your bureau, application and transaction data, kept monotonic wherever your credit policy says they must be. Each decision returns a score, a probability of default and the reason codes an adverse action notice needs.

  • Reason codes on every decision, in your own wording
  • Probability of default calibrated by segment
  • Challenger models in shadow before anything switches

Fraud and AML

See the ring, not just the transaction.

Graph models read accounts, devices, payees and payments together, so a mule ring shows up as a ring. Analysts get the alert, the network behind it and the reasons it scored high, in one view.

  • Real-time scoring on card, e-Transfer and wire
  • Alerts ranked by risk, with the evidence attached
  • Cases mapped to your suspicious transaction reporting

Liquidity forecasting

Cash forecasts with a range you can plan on.

Hierarchical time-series models forecast every account, branch and currency, then reconcile them so the totals add up. Each forecast comes with its P10 to P90 range and the drivers that moved it.

  • Daily forecasts out to 13 weeks
  • Scenarios for rate moves and deposit outflows
  • Drivers explained in treasury terms, not model terms

Governance

Model risk management, built in from the first commit.

Every model carries a model card that fills itself in as you build: data, performance, fairness tests, limitations and approvals. Validators review inside Larch, and every change is logged with who made it and why.

  • Model inventory with tiering and review dates
  • Fairness testing against the groups you choose
  • A full audit trail, exportable for examiners
