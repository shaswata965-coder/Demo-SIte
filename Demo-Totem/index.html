02 — Method

Four weeks, measure to shipped

One engagement, one fixed shape. You get the numbers in week one, so you can stop after it if the numbers are boring.

  1. Week 01

    Measure

    Traces on every hop of the request. Token mix, batch occupancy, cache hit rate, where the GPU is actually idle.

    Ends with — a baseline you can argue with
  2. Week 02

    Model

    Compression candidates run against your evals, not ours. Anything that costs more than a point of quality is dropped here.

    Ends with — two or three live candidates
  3. Week 03

    Rebuild

    The serving path goes back together around the winner: batching, cache policy, routing, autoscaling thresholds.

    Ends with — a shadow deployment
  4. Week 04

    Prove

    Shadow traffic against production until the delta holds for a full week, then a cutover your on-call team runs, not us.

    Ends with — a signed-off cost delta
