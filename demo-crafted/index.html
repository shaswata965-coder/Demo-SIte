03 Method

Four weeks, measure to shipped

One engagement, one fixed shape. The numbers land in week one, so you can stop after it if they are boring.

Week 1

Measure

Traces on every hop. Token mix, batch occupancy, cache hit rate, where the GPU actually idles.

→ a baseline you can argue with

Week 2

Model

Candidates run against your evals. Anything costing more than a point of quality is dropped.

→ two or three live candidates

Week 3

Rebuild

The serving path goes back together around the winner: batching, cache policy, routing, autoscaling.

→ a shadow deployment

Week 4

Prove

Shadow traffic until the delta holds a full week, then a cutover your on-call team runs, not us.

→ a signed-off cost delta
