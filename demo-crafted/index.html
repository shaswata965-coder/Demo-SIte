02 Services

Four levers on the bill

We find the ones with headroom and leave the rest alone.

Measure · two weeks

Inference audit

Two weeks instrumenting your live serving path. You get a costed list of what to change, in order.

We trace every hop: token mix, batch occupancy, KV-cache hit rate, queue depth, and where the GPU actually idles.

  • Output a ranked change list
  • Each line cost delta + the eval it must hold

Model · held to your evals

Model compression

FP8 and 4-bit quantisation, distillation and speculative decoding, each held to your own eval suite.

Candidates run against your evals, not ours. Anything that costs more than a point of quality is dropped before it reaches a GPU budget.

  • Weights FP16 → FP8 → INT4
  • Students distilled per task

Serving · batching and routing

Serving architecture

Continuous batching, paged KV cache, prefix reuse, and routing so the big model only sees what needs it.

Long system prompts are cached once and reused. Easy turns go to a small model; the frontier model sees the few that need it.

  • Batching continuous, admission-controlled
  • Cache paged, prefix-aware
Open

Fleet · right-sizing

Capacity & procurement

Fleet right-sizing, the spot-to-reserved mix, and whether to leave the hosted API at all.

Most fleets are sized for a peak they never see. We size to the measured curve and buy the difference as spot.

03 Method

Four weeks, measure to shipped

One engagement, one fixed shape. The numbers land in week one, so you can stop after it if they are boring.

Week 1

Measure

Traces on every hop. Token mix, batch occupancy, cache hit rate, where the GPU actually idles.

→ a baseline you can argue with

Week 2

Model

Candidates run against your evals. Anything costing more than a point of quality is dropped.

→ two or three live candidates

Week 3

Rebuild

The serving path goes back together around the winner: batching, cache policy, routing, autoscaling.

→ a shadow deployment

Week 4

Prove

Shadow traffic until the delta holds a full week, then a cutover your on-call team runs, not us.

→ a signed-off cost delta

plan · 4 weeks

Phases overlap so nobody waits: modelling starts while the last traces land.
